    FAQ

    Questions
    people ask us.

    My laptop is completely dead. Can it still be repaired?

    Often, yes. A dead laptop is usually caused by a faulty component on the motherboard, such as the power or charging section. With chip-level repair we trace and replace that specific part instead of the whole board.

    Do you repair all laptop brands?

    Yes. We work on all major brands including Dell, HP, Lenovo, Asus, Acer, Apple and more, for both laptops and desktop computers.

    How long does a repair take?

    It depends on the fault. General service and upgrades are usually quick, while chip-level repairs can take longer. We'll give you a time estimate after diagnosis.

    Will an SSD upgrade really make my laptop faster?

    For laptops still running on a hard disk, an SSD is the single biggest speed improvement. Boot-up, app loading and file opening all become much quicker. Adding RAM helps further if you multitask.

    Can you build a custom PC for my budget?

    Yes. Tell us what you'll use it for (office, gaming, editing or study) and your budget, and we'll suggest the right parts, assemble and test it.
